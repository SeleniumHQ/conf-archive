agenda

SeleniumConf & AppiumConf 2026

Valencia, Spain

Where

Veles e Vents

Edificio Veles e Vents La Marina de València, 46024 Valencia

SeleniumConf & AppiumConf Valencia 2026 will be hosted at the Veles e Vents event building, located in the Marina of Valencia. This emblematic venue, with its modern design and excellent facilities, reflects the Mediterranean spirit and provides an ideal space for automation professionals to connect, learn, and innovate. Hotel Primus Valencia will be the official hotel of the conference. Attendees can contact comercial@hotelprimusvalencia.com and mention the conference to receive a 10% discount on their stay.

Veles e Vents Hotel Primus

Workshops - May 6

Hands-on, instructor-led sessions with limited seats. Workshop tickets include access to the main conference (May 7–8).

learn more

Description

Explore the upcoming capabilities in Selenium 5 and what they mean for real automation projects. You’ll work through practical examples and understand how the new features impact architecture, reliability, and maintainability.

Instructor

Titus Fortner — Selenium core contributor and project leadership member, known for helping teams build scalable and diagnosable automation frameworks.

Description

Learn how to build and scale mobile automation using Appium combined with MCP-powered development workflows. This workshop focuses on practical setup, code generation, debugging, and improving productivity across Android and iOS automation.

Instructors

Sai Krishna V — Director of Engineering at TestMu AI (formerly LambdaTest) and long-time Appium contributor and community leader.

Srinivasan Sekar — Director of Engineering at TestMu AI (formerly LambdaTest), open-source contributor across Appium, Selenium, and WebdriverIO.

Description

Understand how to deploy, operate, and scale Selenium Grid for real-world test execution. You’ll explore CI integration, infrastructure patterns, and approaches for reliable parallel execution across environments.

Instructor

Việt Nguyễn Đức — Lead Quality Engineer at Katalon and contributor to Selenium Grid tooling, Kubernetes deployments, and scalable test infrastructure.

Description

Build a solid foundation in Playwright and progress toward advanced automation patterns. The session covers architecture decisions, test stability, and how teams use Playwright effectively in production environments.

Instructor

Stefan Judis — Developer Relations at Checkly, open-source contributor, and experienced educator focused on modern web testing and performance.

Description

Learn how teams use Cypress to test modern web applications in real projects. This workshop focuses on reliable test design, debugging strategies, and practical workflows for front-end-heavy applications.

Instructor

Filip Hric — International speaker and educator known for teaching Cypress and modern testing practices to developers and QA teams worldwide.

Description

A practical workshop to understand mobile automation architecture and reduce friction in real implementations. Learn how Appium and WebdriverIO work together and how to avoid common pitfalls in real projects.

Instructor

Wim Selles — Director at TechChamps and core contributor to WebdriverIO, with extensive experience helping organizations adopt mobile automation at scale.

Description

Learn how to integrate security testing into automation workflows across the software lifecycle. The session focuses on practical strategies, tooling, and collaboration between QA and security teams.

Instructor

Sara Martinez Giner — SDET specializing in cybersecurity testing, with over a decade of experience integrating security practices into automation and QA processes.

Meet the speakers

Voices from five continents, open-source maintainers, engineering leaders, and practitioners working with automation every day.

You’ll hear from contributors to Selenium, Appium, WebdriverIO, and WebDriver BiDi, alongside experts from companies such as Google, Amazon, Sony Interactive Entertainment, The New York Times, SUSE, Broadcom, Mars, and many more.
