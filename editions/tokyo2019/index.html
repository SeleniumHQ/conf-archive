ABOUT
SeleniumConf Tokyo is a two-day conference for test automation professionals on April 18 & 19 preceded by a full day of workshops on April 17.
You can look forward to inspiring and insightful talks, in-depth Selenium training workshops, fantastic networking opportunities in the 'hallway track', and so much more.
