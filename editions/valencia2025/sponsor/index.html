    Ministry of Testing

    Welcome to the MoTaverse!

    Ministry of Testing is a community of practice for software testing professionals. We believe the software testing, quality engineering, and quality assurance professions deserve a space, a community, a network, and the possibility to expand.

    Our mission is to advance our industry by any positive means possible.

    We practice this by having community at the heart of our philosophy for delivering:

    • community contributions in the form of articles, talks, testing trends, conversations, podcasts, courses, memories, memes and more!
    • our (now annual) TestBash conference
    • software testing certifications
    • a cared for and active community

    Ultimately our goal is to thrive together. We exist because of the community and always hope they are better off with the Ministry of Testing.
    We are a small and independent company with no outside investors.

    QA Minds

    QA Minds: A Global Community for Spanish-Speaking Software Testers

    QA Minds is a vibrant community dedicated to connecting and supporting Spanish-speaking software testers worldwide. Focused on fostering growth and promoting quality in software products, QA Minds offers diverse resources, events, and services to help testers stay ahead.

    Through social media, QA Minds shares articles, tutorials, and podcasts covering various testing topics. Regular webinars and meetups provide opportunities for testers to network and learn from industry experts, while the weekly podcast “Miércoles de QA Minders” features insights and stories from testing professionals.

    The annual Hands-On Testing conference is a flagship event, attracting testers globally for three days of workshops, sessions, and networking focused on practical testing techniques and trends.

    Welcoming and inclusive, QA Minds thrives on its members’ passion for advancing software testing. By sharing knowledge and experiences, the community aims to elevate the quality of software products everywhere. Join QA Minds and become part of a mission to improve software testing for all.
