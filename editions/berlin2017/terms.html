Terms & Conditions

Transferring your place

We’ll happily transfer your place to a lucky colleague or friend if you need to. Please let us know as soon as possible and ideally no later than 25 September 2017 – email hello@whiteoctoberevents.co.uk.

If for any reason you can’t attend at all, you’ll need to let us know no later than 11 September 2017. Refunds will not be made after this date.

Photos & footage

We photograph and film all of our events and by registering you give us permission to use any images or footage taken of you on the day. We also reserve the right to reproduce quotes and feedback which will be fully credited.

Accessibility

If you have any requirements you think we should be aware of, please let us know.

Social media

We reserve the right to reproduce quotes and feedback submitted in a public sphere, which will be fully credited.

Programme

From time to time a speaker may drop out of our conference programme as we’ve advertised it and we reserve the right to substitute them for another equally awesome individual. We know you won’t be disappointed!

Privacy

We take your privacy very seriously and will not sell or share your personal information with sponsors or third parties. Sponsors may request to gather your details on the day of the conference but your permission will be sought and the information gathered from you directly.

Code of Conduct

Every attendee, sponsor and speaker, by virtue of purchasing a ticket or attending, agrees to comply in full with our conference code of conduct.
