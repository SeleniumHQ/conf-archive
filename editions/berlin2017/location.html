Location

Conference venue

The Urania Berlin

The venue for the first SeleniumConf Berlin is the Urania Berlin. Located in the heart of the western part of Berlin, the Urania has long been a leading centre of dialogue between science, the humanities and the public. In October 2017, the centre will play host to two days of presentations by Selenium and automated testing experts and practitioners from around the world.

Transport

By Car

Parking spaces are available for The Urania at Kleiststrasse 13, 200m from the venue.

By Bus

The Urania has great links to public transport, with eight buses servicing the An der Urania bus stop outside the venue. Buses: 106, 187, M19, M29, M46, N1, N2, N26.

By Rail

Two metro stations are within walking distance of the venue.

The U1, U2 and U3 stop at Wittenbergplatz station, 350m from The Urania.

The U1, U2, U3 and U4 stop at Nollendorfplatz station, 500m from The Urania.

By Plane

The venue is located approximately 20 minutes by taxi or 30 minutes by public transport from Berlin Tegel Airport.

It is 25 minutes by taxi or 1 hour by public transport from Berlin Schönefeld Airport.
