The SeleniumConf Berlin 2017 Scholarship Scheme

27 April 2017

We’d love for everyone to be able to attend SeleniumConf Berlin, but we know that some people’s personal or financial circumstances will make this difficult, which is why we’re setting aside a number of free tickets as part of our scholarship scheme. The scheme is open for all to apply to, and chosen applicants will receive access to all content, food, drink and all the extras. In some special cases, we are able to cover travel and accommodation.
