Accommodations

The “official” Selenium Conference hotel is The Strand Palace Hotel, located at 372 Strand, London, WC2R 0JJ. This hotel is walking distance from the venue, and attendees of the Selenium Conference can book rooms at the following rates:

Club Single £108.00
Club Twin/Double £133.00
Club King £148.00
Executive King £168.00

To book a room at these rates you must either call the hotel at 020 73794737 or send an email to reservations@strandpalacehotel.co.uk. Please quote IET Savoy Place Selenium Conference as a reference. Those rates are exclusive of VAT which is charged at 20%.
