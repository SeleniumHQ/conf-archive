Convince your manager

Looking forward to SeleniumConf, but need approval from your manager? We’ve got you covered! To help you make the case, we've pulled together the main points for attending and an email template you can adapt as you see fit.

What's special about SeleniumConf London?

Here are some of the reasons why people love coming to SeleniumConf – you can include some of these in your email.

Experience reports and what's new in testing

Every SeleniumConf event brings together an exciting line-up of speakers, working on some of the most interesting projects around. It’s important to stay up-to-date and learn what’s possible these days, and where testing is heading –– from how to deploy Selenium 4 to communicating more efficiently. You'll be able to spot where you can improve as a team and implement solutions right away.

Networking (in a non-awkward way!)

The friendly spirit of the conference makes it easy to meet and interact with other testers and make valuable connections. There’s nothing like being in a room with hundreds of like-minded people to uncover ideas and spark inspiration! You will have access to the best and brightest experts in the industry, and if your company is hiring, you can find talented people to work with you.

Got a question for the speakers?

Most of the conference speakers, including committers, are participating in the Office Hours sessions throughout the conference, so you'll be able to ask your questions directly and get their take on the testing related problems you might be struggling with.

How will this benefit the rest of the company?

When you get back from SeleniumConf, you can schedule a session with the rest of the team to present what you've learned. All the speaker presentations and talk videos are made available to attendees after the event, so you'll also be able to share these with the company together with your learnings and experience report.
