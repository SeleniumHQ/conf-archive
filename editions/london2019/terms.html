Transferring your place

We’ll happily transfer your place to a lucky colleague or friend if you need to. Please let us know as soon as possible and ideally no later than 23 September 2019 – email.

If for any reason you can’t attend at all, you’ll need to let us know no later than 5 September 2019. Refunds will not be made after this date.
