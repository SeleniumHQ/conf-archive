Extras - what else is happening

As well as an impressive two-day, two-track programme, you can look forward to plenty of extras outside the sessions to help you make the most of the conference. We also have The International Networking Mixer hosted by Sauce Labs on Day 1!

Four practical workshops

We have four workshops scheduled on 9 October to offer the perfect opportunity to learn in an informal setting - and get individual feedback and support from your tutors:

  • Diving into Selenium
  • Selenium Grid
  • Fix a Bug, Become a Committer
  • Advanced Appium

Lightning talks

We'd love to hear about the projects you're working on! Sign up on Day 1 to share your testing story in a super fast 3-minute talk.

Office hours

Remember, all of our speakers will be taking part in office hours sessions throughout the conference. No need to pre-book, just drop by to have a chat or to ask questions.

Fantastic sponsors

This is an opportunity for you to meet our lovely sponsors and enter their prize draws or get some cool swag. Come and say hi during breaks!

Chillout space & quiet zone

In need of a break from the hustle and bustle of the event? We’re setting up a chillout zone and a separate quiet zone for you to relax and recharge your batteries.

International Networking Mixer supported by Sauce Labs

We have attendees from 32 countries around the world - travelling from far away places like Japan, Australia, Brazil and Canada! All attendees are invited to The International Networking Mixer hosted by our Premier sponsor Sauce Labs at the end of the Day 1 to celebrate all things testing, taste some international cuisines, with alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages provided.
