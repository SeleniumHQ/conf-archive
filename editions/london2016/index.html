SeleniumConf UK 2016

SeleniumConf UK is this year's flagship Selenium conference, bringing together Selenium developers & enthusiasts from around the world to share ideas, socialise, and work together on advancing the present and future success of the project.

London, UK | Nov 14-16th

In A Nutshell
