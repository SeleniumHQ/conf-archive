Directions To Conference Venue

By Plane

The venue is situated in Blackfriars, within an hour’s reach of Heathrow, Gatwick, Luton and City airports.

By Car

From the Victoria Embankment, there is easy access to the Corporation of London 300 space pay and display car park beside the venue.

By Underground

The nearest tube stops are Blackfriars (2 minute walk) and Mansion House (6 minute walk) on the District/Circle line. Alternatively you can reach the venue from St Paul’s on the Central line (4 minute walk).

By Train

Blackfriars Mainline is the nearest station to the venue, and can be reached on Thameslink from any station between Bedford, Brighton and Ashford. Follow the covered walkway direct to The Mermaid.

By Bus

Bus numbers 8, 25, 242 and 50 stop at Cheapside, and numbers 4, 11, 15, 17, 23, 26, 76 and 172 stop at St Paul’s Cathedral. Both stops are a short walk from the venue.

By Bicycle

The venue has allocated parking spaces for cycles.
